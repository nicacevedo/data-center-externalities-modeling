Title: Water Efficiency at Facebook's Prineville Data Center
Posted: 2013-08-07 by leed (Facebook / Open Compute)
Acquired: 2026-09-02 for PRN1 Q2-2012 public validation (water-cycle / WUE / recapture only).

KEY EXTRACTS (verbatim from live page):

"we're releasing our first water usage effectiveness (WUE) measurements and information on how we've achieved what we think is a strong level of efficiency in water use for cooling in the first building at our Prineville, Ore., data center (which we'll call Prineville 1 here)."

"After a year of operation, which required our retrofitting the building management system and adding water metering, we've found Prineville 1's Q2 2012 WUE to be 0.22 L/kWh."

"WUE measures water used for cooling a data center only; it doesn't measure water used for plumbing or offices"

"The WUE for the second building at our Prineville data center should be available next year, when that building is online."

"Our measurements indicate that 75% of the water brought into the data center is used for cooling (3 out of 4 parts of RO water are used for product), while the remaining 25% is blown down."

"Approximately 85% of the misted water evaporates into the supply air stream. The remaining 15% is recaptured in a mist eliminator that is downstream of the misting nozzles. The recaptured RO water is put through a micron filter and UV lamp and then brought back to the RO water storage tanks"

"The OCP mechanical system has no chillers or cooling towers."
