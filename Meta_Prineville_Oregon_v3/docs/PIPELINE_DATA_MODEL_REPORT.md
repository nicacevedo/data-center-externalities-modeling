## 1. Pipeline overview

The implemented pipeline reconstructs a **weather-driven facility** from **annual public campus totals**, then places those totals in **regional water and grid context**. It does **not** recover hourly IT telemetry, does **not** attribute Oregon generators to the campus, and does **not** run a groundwater or DID/causal model.

Layers actually executed:

1. **Targets.** `src/build_targets.py` curates Meta annual electricity (2011–2024), water withdrawal (2014–2024), location Scope 2, and operational GHG into `data/canonical/meta_prineville_annual.csv`.
2. **Weather.** Canonical KS39/KRDM with KBDN tertiary gap-only fallback → `data/processed/weather_hourly.csv` (122,736 unique local-calendar hours, 2011–2024).
3. **Gray-box physics.** `src/prineville_graybox.py` maps IT power + weather → cooling mode, PUE, raw evaporation.
4. **Conditional reconstruction.** `src/conditional_reconstruction.py` closes annual facility electricity with one latent IT-power scale per year and predicts water with a train-only multiplicative scale on raw evaporation.
5. **Stochastic proxy.** `src/stochastic_conditional_simulation.py` is a **generative scenario** with a separate annual water **prediction** horse-race (energy-only selected).
6. **Context, not coupling.** OWRD City/POD, USGS HUC12 IWA/use, EIA-930 PACW, FERC Form 714 PacifiCorp-West monthly / East+West shape, eGRID NWPP, Oregon CAMPD/EIA, DEQ backup, Crook County permits, documentary/regulatory identity and legal/network context.

Annual electricity agreement is **closure, not prediction**. IWA `availab = strflow - consum` is an **identity, not hydrologic validation**. City production is **not** Meta delivery. Direct POD is **not** total Meta withdrawal.

## 4. Model quantity → source/proxy mapping

Full table: [`model_quantity_registry.csv`](../outputs/pipeline_report/model_quantity_registry.csv). Dependency diagram: [PNG](../outputs/pipeline_report/model_quantity_dependency.png) generated from [`model_io_edges.csv`](../outputs/pipeline_report/model_io_edges.csv).

Each quantity has a `boundary_id` from a small controlled vocabulary. This makes mechanically visible that Meta campus withdrawal is not City production, not Vitesse/Facebook POD use, and not USGS public-supply WD; USGS local-use quantities are not routed IWA quantities; Meta facility electricity is not PACW BA demand; PACW/eGRID are not Oregon generator output. There is no boundary algebra beyond these two columns.

Provenance classes used (exactly one per row): `reported / measured / derived / fitted / simulated / scenario / proxy / unavailable`.

### Workload arrivals (`A_{c,t}`)

- **What is it?** New work arriving in interval t. No public scheduler traces exist; the stochastic module draws scale-free Cox-process counts.
- **Where does it come from?** none (no telemetry); src/stochastic_conditional_simulation.py
- **How is it computed?** Poisson counts from diurnal/weekly/AR log-intensity; not recovered arrivals
- **Assumptions?** base_rate=72; mixed interactive/batch Cox scenario; scale-free by construction
- **Provenance:** `scenario` (implemented as generative scenario only)
- **Accounting boundary:** `SCENARIO_WORKLOAD` — Generative Cox-process arrivals; not campus scheduler telemetry.
- **Validation?** structural queue conservation only; no arrival telemetry
- **Confidence:** low (scenario, not observation). Hourly IT workload traces are not public.
### IT power (`P^{IT}_{i,t}`)

- **What is it?** Electricity used by IT equipment. Conditional reconstruction uses one latent constant-within-year IT-power scale so gray-box facility energy closes to reported annual facility MWh. Stochastic uses a workload shape then the same annual closure.
- **Where does it come from?** Meta annual facility electricity + NOAA weather + gray-box
- **How is it computed?** P_IT_scale = E_fac_reported / sum_t P_fac(weather, P_IT=1 MW); hourly P_IT = scale (conditional) or scale × shape (stochastic)
- **Assumptions?** constant IT power within a year in the conditional baseline; gray-box linear in IT power; not observed PDU telemetry
- **Provenance:** `fitted` (implemented as fitted latent (conditional) / simulated-then-closed (stochastic))
- **Accounting boundary:** `META_LATENT_IT` — Latent IT power: annual scale fitted to campus facility electricity, or scenario shape then the same closure. Not PDU telemetry.
- **Validation?** annual electricity residual ≈ 0 by construction (outputs/conditional_annual_compare.csv)
- **Confidence:** low for hourly path; medium that annual IT energy is in a physically consistent PUE range. Hourly IT load is not public. Within-year variation is assumed (conditional) or scenario (stochastic).
### Facility electricity (annual reported) (`E^{fac}`)

- **What is it?** Meta-disclosed Prineville annual facility electricity. Hard observation for calibration.
- **Where does it come from?** META_2016_DISCLOSURE / META_2019_DISCLOSURE / META_2025_INDEX via build_targets.py
- **How is it computed?** avg MW = MWh / hours_in_year
- **Assumptions?** disclosed site annual total is the campus facility electricity used by the model
- **Provenance:** `reported` (implemented)
- **Accounting boundary:** `META_CAMPUS` — Meta-disclosed Prineville annual facility electricity. Not PACW BA demand.
- **Validation?** source-vintage notes in meta_prineville_annual.csv
- **Confidence:** high as disclosed annual total. No monthly/hourly campus electricity observations.
### PUE (`PUE`)

- **What is it?** P_fac/P_IT from the gray-box. Annual modeled PUE ≈ 1.060. 2011 design full-load PUE 1.07 is a design/assumption consistency (falsification) check, not independent validation.
- **Where does it come from?** gray-box
- **How is it computed?** PUE = P_fac / P_IT
- **Assumptions?** bottom-up PUE; not an independent input
- **Provenance:** `derived` (implemented as derived KPI)
- **Accounting boundary:** `META_LATENT_IT` — Derived P_fac/P_IT from the gray-box on the latent IT + reconstructed facility boundary.
- **Validation?** 2011 modeled annual PUE 1.060 vs 1.07
- **Confidence:** medium vs 2011 design; low as a claim of true hourly PUE. No simultaneous IT and facility metering.
### Direct water consumption (`W^{dir,cons}`)

- **What is it?** Water not returned to the local system. Not identified: no discharge/sewer series and Meta tables used here are withdrawal.
- **Where does it come from?** not identified
- **How is it computed?** would require withdrawal minus discharge or a documented consumptive fraction; neither is in code
- **Assumptions?** n/a
- **Provenance:** `unavailable` (unavailable)
- **Accounting boundary:** `NOT_IDENTIFIED` — Campus consumption is not identified.
- **Validation?** none
- **Confidence:** none. Site consumption is not publicly reported as a separate campus series in the canonical table.
### Direct water withdrawal (Meta annual) (`W^{dir,with}`)

- **What is it?** Meta-disclosed Prineville annual water withdrawal. Hard observation. Not monthly.
- **Where does it come from?** META_2016_DISCLOSURE / META_2019_DISCLOSURE / META_2025_INDEX
- **How is it computed?** unit conversion to m³ in build_targets.py; 2011-2013 not publicly reported at site level
- **Assumptions?** disclosed withdrawal is the campus water target used for water models
- **Provenance:** `reported` (implemented)
- **Accounting boundary:** `META_CAMPUS` — Meta-disclosed annual campus withdrawal. Not City production and not Vitesse/Facebook POD use.
- **Validation?** source status in meta_prineville_annual.csv
- **Confidence:** high as disclosed annual withdrawal. No monthly Meta water meters. 2011-2013 missing.
### Hourly/annual water-withdrawal proxy (conditional) (`s × W^{evap,raw}`)

- **What is it?** Train-only global multiplicative scale on raw evaporation. Selected model is global (not one-break). Holdout years use the frozen train scale.
- **Where does it come from?** raw evaporation × fitted scale
- **How is it computed?** s = exp(mean log(W_obs/W_raw)) on train years with water; W_hat = s W_raw; BIC vs one-break
- **Assumptions?** raw evaporation shape is a weather driver; the fitted scale maps simplified raw-evaporation physics onto the broader Meta withdrawal accounting boundary and is not a physical cooling multiplier or mass-balance parameter
- **Provenance:** `proxy` (implemented)
- **Accounting boundary:** `META_CAMPUS` — Predicted/proxy campus withdrawal from gray-box evaporation times a train-only scale; still the Meta campus water boundary, not a meter.
- **Validation?** water_pct_error in conditional_annual_compare.csv; holdout 2023 +126.7%, 2024 +100.8%; holdout MAPE 113.8%
- **Confidence:** low (holdout fails; not a validated predictor of the 2023-2024 regime). Proxy is not a meter. Poor holdout means the frozen scale does not travel. Current fitted/holdout numbers are applied at report generation from canonical outputs.
### Municipal / City production (`City POD production`)

- **Where does it come from?** OWRD_WUR_QUERY
- **How is it computed?** accepted Report-ID/source groups only; candidates excluded from primary
- **Assumptions?** City production is not Meta delivery
- **Provenance:** `reported` (implemented)
- **Accounting boundary:** `PRINEVILLE_PWS` — OWRD accepted City of Prineville municipal POD production. Not Meta campus delivery.
- **Validation?** outputs/qc/water_context_qa.csv; owrd_water_model_validation_checks.csv
- **Confidence:** high as OWRD reported City production; not usable as Meta water. Do not compute prediction error vs Meta withdrawal.

## 6. Core equations and assumptions

### Gray-box (`src/prineville_graybox.py`)

Assumed parameters (code priors, **not reported Meta facts** except the 2011 technology class):

- `supply_target_C = 25`
- `return_air_C = 35` (**declared but unused** in `simulate()`)
- `evap_effectiveness = 0.85`
- `server_deltaT_C = 12`
- `dry_air_cp_J_kgK = 1006`
- `fan_fraction_of_it = 0.025`
- `other_facility_fraction_of_it = 0.035`
- evaporative auxiliary `0.005 × P_IT × spray`

Equations actually coded:

1. **IT heat / airflow:** \(m_\mathrm{air} = P^{IT} \times 10^6 / (c_p \Delta T_\mathrm{server})\).
2. **Full-evap outlet:** \(T_\mathrm{full} = T_{db} - \varepsilon \max(T_{db}-T_{wb},0)\).
3. **Supply:** outdoor if \(T_{db} \le 25\); else 25 °C if reachable; else \(T_\mathrm{full}\).
4. **Evaporative water:** humidity-ratio increase at constant moist-air enthalpy; \(\mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{h} = (\mathrm{kg/s}) \times 3.6\).
5. **Facility power:** \(P_\mathrm{fac} = P^{IT} + 0.025 P^{IT} + 0.035 P^{IT} + 0.005 P^{IT} \mathrm{spray}\).
6. **PUE:** \(P_\mathrm{fac} / P^{IT}\).
7. **Modes:** `outside_air_or_winter_mix` / `partial_evap` / `full_evap`.

2011 design benchmark used only as a **design/assumption consistency or falsification check**, not independent validation: full-load PUE 1.07, WUE 0.31 L/kWh. Current modeled 2011 annual PUE = **1.0601**. Parameter inventory: [`model_parameter_registry.csv`](../outputs/pipeline_report/model_parameter_registry.csv). `return_air_C` is **declared but unused**. One-at-a-time sensitivity of documented-range overhead fractions is [Figure 7](../outputs/pipeline_report/figures/fig07_graybox_parameter_sensitivity.png); it is an assumption audit, **not a confidence interval**.

The gray-box remains a **simplified common-architecture baseline**. Crook County PRN1 permits document a meaningful late-2023 commissioning → early-2024 post-addition infrastructure transition (chilled-water / CRAH / chiller scope at PRN1). That evidence is a plausible source of model misspecification and future scenario structure. It is **not** used retrospectively to introduce a post-2023 cooling regime, retune parameters, or add a breakpoint to improve the 2023–2024 water holdout.

### Conditional reconstruction

1. Annual electricity **closure** via one latent IT-power scale (linear gray-box).
2. Weather-driven hourly gray-box **shape**.
3. Water **raw-evaporation proxy**.
4. Global vs one-break multiplicative water scale; **global selected** (\(s=8.246503\), BIC=-11.43).
5. Log-scale fitting (geometric mean).
6. BIC with a required improvement of 2 to prefer one-break (3 parameters).
7. Training through 2022; **2023–2024 holdout**.

### Annual water prediction candidates (stochastic workflow)

Pre-registered nonnegative no-intercept models, expanding-window one-step MAPE on train, then freeze:

- energy-only (**selected mechanistic candidate**, \(\beta_E \approx 0.3643\,\mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{MWh}\), rolling MAPE 34.25%). Selected among pre-registered mechanistic/covariate candidates; **not** a claim of best overall predictor.
- evaporation-only (not selected)
- energy + evaporation NNLS (evaporation coefficient currently 0; still not selected)

### Stochastic proxy

Cox-process arrivals, AR latent intensity, Poisson counts, Gamma work sizes, aggregate queue, utilization → IT-power shape, annual facility-energy scaling, uncertain facility-overhead priors, retrospective water-shape mixture, scenario ensemble (seed 20260812, 32 sims/year, `mixed_cox`). This is **not** recovered workload telemetry. Stochastic water/PUE intervals are **heuristic scenario ensembles under assumed workload priors**, not calibrated confidence intervals with demonstrated coverage.

### Carbon

- Meta annual **reported location Scope 2**.
- **eGRID NWPP × Meta MWh** is a **methodology/accounting consistency benchmark**, not fully independent external validation (both sides use Meta campus MWh).
- **PACW EIA consumed CO2** as regional hourly **relative shape** (optional).
- Fuel/import score: **sensitivity proxy only**.
- None of these is a Meta-specific marginal-emissions model.

### Change-point screening

`src/change_point_seed.py` ranks piecewise-linear SSE reductions. Statistical candidates only (train-only water/intensity peak at 2020). Not a physical technology-change claim.

Scorecard: [`validation_scorecard.csv`](../outputs/pipeline_report/validation_scorecard.csv). Evidence types A–F are separated on purpose. Type E is **not** “independent external validation”: eGRID is a methodology/accounting consistency benchmark; 2011 PUE is a design/assumption consistency check.

**Water — primary predictive figure:** [Figure 3](../outputs/pipeline_report/figures/fig03_water_model_accuracy.png).

The complete eight-predictor comparison remains in [`water_holdout_baseline_compare.csv`](../outputs/pipeline_report/water_holdout_baseline_compare.csv).

Displayed models:

- Conditional: `W_hat_y = s * V_raw_evap_y`, with frozen `s = 8.246503`.
- Selected mechanistic energy-only: `W_hat_y = beta_E * E_fac_y`.
- Frozen training mean: mean 2014–2022 observed withdrawal; not entered into mechanistic selection.

The first two are **not** dynamic time-series models. 2023–2024 was unused in fitting/selection. Holdout \(N=2\) is a diagnostic, not strong statistical evidence. The naive mean currently performs much better on holdout.

The current water specifications **perform poorly** on the pre-specified 2023–2024 holdout and are **not validated predictors** of the recent operating regime. With only two holdout years, this is a **strong predictive diagnostic failure**, not a formal statistical proof or falsification test.

The **selected mechanistic candidate** is `energy_null` (expanding-window train MAPE **34.25%**; frozen-NNLS holdout MAPE **135.2%**). That label means it won among the pre-registered mechanistic/covariate candidates. It is **not** a claim that it is the best predictor overall. The frozen training-mean baseline was **not** entered into selection and currently has holdout MAPE **24.5%**, much better on these two years.

The conditional scale \(s=8.246503\) is an **empirically fitted mapping** from simplified raw-evaporation physics to the broader withdrawal accounting boundary — **not** a physical cooling multiplier or mass-balance parameter.

MAE, MAPE, percent error, and skill versus the training-mean baseline are **informative diagnostics rather than strong statistical evidence**. Naive baselines (training mean, training median, 2022 persistence) are frozen from training observations through 2022 and were **not** entered into model selection.

Expanding-window one-step MAPE for the selected energy-only candidate is a **train-period selection metric**. Full-training fitted/historical values are **not** those expanding-window predictions. 2023–2024 remain untouched holdout predictions.

## Documentation vs executable code

If documentation disagrees with code, **code behavior wins**. Current discrepancies:

- **source_manifest.csv completeness.** Documentation: data/source_manifest.csv lists 34 sources (Meta, OWRD, NOAA, EIA-930, eGRID, USGS, OHA, City, ASR PDFs). Code/files: Executable pipeline also uses data/raw/{campd,eia860,eia923,eia_cooling,epa_eia_crosswalk,deq_air,deq_ghg} and Crook County permit tables. Resolution: Treat data/source_manifest.csv as a legacy/local acquisition manifest. The complete executable inventory is src/pipeline_report_catalog.py::source_inventory() and outputs/pipeline_report/data_source_inventory.csv. Lineage validation requires every source ID on registered source→quantity edges to exist in that executable inventory.
- **NOAA_GH_KRDM_FILES bundling.** Documentation: source_manifest status says Global Hourly files are not bundled because remote CSV access may be environment-dependent. Code/files: data/processed/weather_hourly.csv exists for 2011-2024 and is required by reconstruction. Resolution: Processed weather is the executable input. Raw yearly CSVs may or may not be present under data/raw/noaa/.
- **NOAA_ISD station-ID verification.** Documentation: source_manifest NOAA_ISD status still says station-ID verification is needed. Code/files: config/prineville.yaml and download_noaa_global_hourly.py use KRDM 72692024230. Resolution: Code uses KRDM. Manifest status is stale.
- **a2 WUE curves in glossary.** Documentation: modeling/glossary_mapping.tex defines Gupta et al. fixed-approach/fixed-cold-water WUE curves. Code/files: src/prineville_graybox.py implements air-side evaporative humidification, not those polynomials. Resolution: Quantity Q_A2_WUE is unavailable. Implemented water physics is the gray-box.
- **return_air_C prior.** Documentation: Params.return_air_C = 35.0 is declared in prineville_graybox.py. Code/files: simulate() never reads return_air_C. Resolution: Documented as unused in the quantity registry and gray-box model notes.
- **README Stage 8 validation hierarchy.** Documentation: README.md Stage 8 lists 'annual held-out electricity/water/location-Scope-2 prediction'. Code/files: src/conditional_reconstruction.py closes electricity every year; location Scope 2 is closed in the stochastic proxy; only water has a chronological holdout prediction. Resolution: Electricity and Scope 2 agreement are closure. Water 2023-2024 errors are the predictive holdout. Report command documents this; README Stage 8 item 3 should not be read as electricity forecast skill.
