# OCWD method transfer contract

Status: frozen for the Andhra Pradesh preflight on 2026-09-04.

OCWD is evidence about an empirical model class and identification protocol.
It is not a parameter donor for Andhra Pradesh.

## Transferable

- Sequential no-pumping/background, managed-forcing, spatial-forcing, and
  minimal-network comparisons.
- Chronological and full held-out-location validation.
- Train-only preprocessing, validation-only complexity selection, and a
  protected final test.
- Well/group-level uncertainty rather than treating repeated observations as
  independent.
- Season-preserving temporal and hydrogeologically constrained spatial
  placebos.
- A complexity gate: spatial/network structure is attempted only after simpler
  forcing representations earn it out of sample.
- Reserved tracer/intervention validation that remains outside fitting and
  selection until the model is frozen.

## Not transferable

- Fitted A or B matrices.
- Storage coefficients, conductances, transmissivities, or network weights.
- Recharge, injection, or pumping-response coefficients.
- OCWD spatial-kernel choices as Andhra Pradesh physical constants.
- OCWD aquifer geometry, layers, boundary conditions, time scales, or source
  definitions.

Every physical quantity entering an Andhra Pradesh groundwater model must be
estimated or fixed from local authoritative evidence. The presence or success
of an OCWD experiment cannot make `AP9` pass.
