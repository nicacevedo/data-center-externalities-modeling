# Andhra Pradesh planning spatial-unit decision

`AP_SPATIAL_UNIT_STATUS = UNRESOLVED`

Groundwater assessment units are the preliminary preferred planning basis
because CGWB/AP’s 2024 resource assessment reports recharge, extraction, and
stage at 679 assessment-unit/mandal units. They are not yet selected: canonical
polygons, stable IDs, vintage, 748-microbasin mapping, and crosswalks to the
public monitoring wells, agriculture, municipalities, grid, and candidate data
centers were not available in a machine-readable bundle.

The 2024 assessment describes village-level water balances aggregated through
748 microbasins to 679 assessment units. The 2024-25 yearbook separately lists
667 revenue mandals, so an authoritative versioned administrative/assessment
crosswalk is mandatory; numerical proximity is not identity.

No unit was chosen using optimization outcomes.
